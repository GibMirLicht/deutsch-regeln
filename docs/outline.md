# Rule tree outline (draft)

Status: **draft, awaiting owner approval** (PLAN stage 2).

The full numbered tree of grammar topics, A1–C1. Each node is one article with exactly one place in the tree.
Format: **number** German title — *Russian title* · level. Parentheses give the scope or marker words.

## Conventions

- **Every node is an article.** A parent node is a short intro to its branch (what it covers, links to children); leaves hold the rules.
- **Level** is the level at which the topic is first needed. For a parent, it is the lowest level among its children. Level is a label and a search filter; it does not hide nodes or change the order.
- **Numbers come from the order.** In stage 4 the numbering is generated from the order of the content folders, so inserting a node renumbers its siblings automatically; numbers are not stored in articles.
- **Overview topics** (section 12) are cross-cutting: they collect what is spread over several sections (e.g. Dativ: verbs, prepositions, article and adjective endings) and link to the specific articles. Specific articles link back to their overview.
- **No duplicates.** Where a topic could live in two places, it lives in one and the other links to it (e.g. relative pronouns live in 1.7 Relativsätze, not in 4 Pronomen; *als ob* lives in 1.6.10, not in 2.8 Konjunktiv II).
- **Office / IT / business** emphasis shows in examples (tier `office`) and in a few dedicated nodes: 2.14, 6.4.2, 9.5, 10.5, 10.6, 11.
